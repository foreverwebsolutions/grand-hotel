About Us

Experience Elegance at Port Royal's Historical Hotel

Grand Hotel Excelsior, a premier destination along Port Royal Main Road, is the largest resort in Kingston, Jamaica. Being the sole Airport hotel in Kingston, we provide oceanfront accommodations in our 61 rooms and suites, each designed to your comfort.

Grand Hotel offers a full-service Marina, where amenities like fresh water, electricity, diesel fuel, 24-hour security, and customs clearance for visiting yachts await. We're committed to giving you a blend of history, elegance, and convenience, to ensure your stay is not just memorable but exceptional.

Our blend of historic charm and modern comforts creates an unparalleled ambiance. Situated in the historic Port Royal, once nicknamed the "wickedest city on earth," Grand Hotel Excelsior is a testament to the rich history of the area. This historic town was a notorious pirate haven and a bustling center of trade during the 17th century. Parts of the first James Bond movie, Dr. No, were filmed in Port Royal, adding to its allure and cinematic history.

Whether you're exploring the historic sites of Port Royal, relaxing on our beautiful beaches, or enjoying the vibrant culture of Kingston, Grand Hotel Excelsior Port Royal is your gateway to an unforgettable experience in Jamaica

A Legacy of Legends

Experience the Birthplace of James Bond:

At Grand Hotel Excelsior Port Royal our cinematic history adds to our modern design. Our Sean Connery Suite pays homage to the iconic first James Bond film, creating a unique and captivating experience for our guests. Enjoy the subtle remnants of cinematic history that enrich our resort and blend a sense of nostalgia with contemporary comfort.

Discover the Pirate City:

Experience the legacy of Henry Morgan at Grand Hotel Excelsior Port Royal—formerly Morgan's Harbor. Our property pays homage to this legendary figure, with the proud presence of Fort Kilail. Unearth mysteries of a sunken city that thrives beneath the ocean's surface. Explore historic cannons and experience the playful allure of the Giddy House, adding a touch of delightful dizziness to your adventure. Your treasure hunt commences the moment you step into Grand Hotel Excelsior.
