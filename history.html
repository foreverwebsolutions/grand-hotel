History

Nestled within the storied landscape of Port Royal, Jamaica, Grand Hotel Excelsior Port Royal stands as a timeless emblem of hospitality and heritage. Originally known as Morgan’s Harbor, the hotel traces its roots back to the illustrious days of the 17th century when Port Royal flourished as a bustling pirate haven and center of trade in the Caribbean. With its strategic location at the heart of this historic port town, the hotel has witnessed centuries of maritime adventures and cultural exchange, embodying the rich tapestry of Jamaica’s past.

Today, Grand Hotel Excelsior Port Royal blends its fascinating history with modern luxury, offering guests a unique opportunity to immerse themselves in the captivating narratives of the Caribbean. Whether exploring the remnants of Fort Carlisle, strolling along the cobblestone streets of Port Royal, or indulging in the hotel’s exquisite amenities, visitors are invited to embark on a journey through time while experiencing the warm hospitality and charm that define this iconic destination.
