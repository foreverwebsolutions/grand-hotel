On The Tracks Of James Bond

The 007GB Club has been successfully serving the James Bond community since 2022.

A not-for-profit club dedicated to the wonderful world of 007, it offers a global community of James Bond enthusiasts a series of annual events to celebrate their shared interests and to raise monies for UNICEF, Sir Roger Moore’s charity for which he worked as a Goodwill Ambassador.

Having raised over £55,000 for UNICEF as of July 2025, the events include their triumphant gala dinner event held aboard the HMS Belfast in London and where they presented director John Glen with his ‘Bond & Beyond’ lifetime achievement award, and the highly ambitious and hugely successful Concorde gala where they presented two lifetime achievement awards to Thunderball star Luciana Paluzzi and stunt arranger and director Vic Armstrong.
